
# Marcos Galeote

### Independent Researcher · Interdisciplinary Thinking

I am an independent researcher interested in understanding how the world works, how systems evolve, and how different fields of knowledge connect.

My interests span economics, finance, institutions, technology, business, human behaviour and the structures that shape society.

I believe meaningful research begins with curiosity, develops through rigorous investigation, and grows through the willingness to question one's own assumptions.

---

## About

I use research as a way to explore questions, understand complex phenomena and develop independent perspectives.

My approach combines:

- **Curiosity** — exploring questions across different disciplines.
- **Research** — working with original sources, official documents and verifiable data.
- **Critical thinking** — distinguishing evidence, assumptions and interpretations.
- **Interdisciplinary perspective** — looking for connections between fields without losing methodological rigour.

I am particularly interested in questions that do not fit neatly within a single discipline.

## INDAGINI

**INDAGINI** is my independent research project.

It is a space for investigating ideas, examining evidence and developing research over time.

Its purpose is not to follow a predefined subject, but to pursue meaningful questions wherever they lead — from economics and institutions to technology, science and human systems.

## Research interests

- Economics, finance and business
- Institutions, political and social systems
- Technology and artificial intelligence
- Human behaviour and knowledge
- Complex systems and interdisciplinary research

These are areas of exploration, not fixed boundaries.

## Research principles

- Evidence before conclusions.
- Primary sources whenever possible.
- Transparency about methods and limitations.
- Clear distinction between facts and interpretations.
- Intellectual independence and openness to criticism.
